    SimpleJot

    A simple online notepad. No distractions, no unnecessary features. The text will live in your browser until you decide to delete it, come back anytime to continue writing. You can also download your text as a .txt file after you're finished!

    Tip: You can use SimpleJot when offline!

    Source code: 2nul/simplejot
